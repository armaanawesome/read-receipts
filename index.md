# Read Receipts

A murder mystery told entirely through text threads. You have the victim's
phone. You win only by pinning two messages that cannot both be true, and
proving it.

## Legal

- [Privacy policy](privacy/) -- what the app collects, and every right you have over it
- [Terms of use](terms/) -- your licence to play, refunds, and liability

The short version of the privacy policy: no ads, no analytics, no tracking
SDKs, nothing sold. The game plays in full without an account. With one, a
server holds your email address and which cases you have solved, and you can
delete both from inside the app.
