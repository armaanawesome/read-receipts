# Third-party notices

Read Receipts ships open-source components. Most are under the MIT licence,
which requires that "the above copyright notice and this permission notice
shall be included in all copies or substantial portions of the Software".

A name and a licence *type* does not discharge that. The notice text does, and
this file is where it lives. The in-app panel in Settings > Open-source
licences is the summary; this is the thing the licences actually ask for.

Generated from the installed tree, not written by hand, so it cannot drift
away from what is really in node_modules. Regenerate it when a dependency is
added or upgraded. See docs/LEGAL-REVIEW.md, Count 9.
